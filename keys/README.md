# Keyboard samples

## Acoustic piano: `piano_<MIDI>.mp3`
Excerpts (velocity layer 11 of 16, every minor third A0–C8, summed to mono, trimmed and
faded) from **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by **Alexander Holm**
(https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3, SFZ remap:
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano).

## Electric piano: `rhodes_<MIDI>.mp3`
Excerpts (velocity layer 4 of 5, mono, trimmed and faded) from **jRhodes3d**, a 1977 Rhodes

Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0)**:
